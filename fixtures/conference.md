# SymfonyCon Warsaw 2026

Date: November 26 – 27, 2026
Location: Warsaw (Poland)

November 24-25, 2026: Two days of immersive pre-conference workshops
November 26-27, 2026: Two days of engaging talks across multiple tracks
November 28, 2026: Hackday - Open to everyone (no ticket needed)

Join us for SymfonyCon Warsaw 2026 as we continue bringing the community together for another exceptional edition of
our international event! We're returning to Warsaw, which had hosted the first SymfonyCon in 2013, for an experience
filled with learning, inspiration, and connection.

## Who should attend?
* Symfony Developers: All levels of experience and knowledge welcome
* Symfony Newcomers: To learn about Symfony, and how it will positively impact their everyday life
* PHP developers: Eager to widen their knowledge on frameworks, best practices and quality PHP development
* Drupal developers: Looking to learn more about the Symfony components used internally by Drupal
* IT Professionals: Development managers, devOps and other professionals with an interest in learning more about Symfony

## 5 reasons to come to the conference
* Meet in real-life the people from the Symfony community you're working with / talking to online the rest of the year
* Network with the community
* Discover the latest news about Symfony in prime time
* Ask all the questions you have about Symfony/PHP to the Symfony Core Team
* Have fun in a inclusive and friendly environment in a bright city!

## Venue
The conference takes place at the Hilton Warsaw City, which brings a unique, fresh style of contemporary living to the
downtown business district in Warsaw. The hotel is extremely easy to reach, both by public transport and by car.

Address: Hilton Warsaw Hotel and Convention Centre, Grzybowska 63, 00-844 Warszawa, Poland

Getting there:
* Tram: Muzeum Powstania Warszawskiego and Rondo Daszyńskiego stops via lines 1, 9, 10, 22, 24
* Metro: Rondo Daszyńskiego station (Line M2), a 5 minute walk from the hotel
* Bus: Rondo Daszyńskiego or Muzeum Powstania Warszawskiego stops via lines 106, 109, 178
* Warsaw Chopin Airport: 21 minutes by car (8km)
* Warsaw Train station: 19 minutes by walk (1.5km)
* Parking: Available on site at 180 PLN per day (Monday to Thursday) and 150 PLN per day (Friday to Sunday)
